## Higgsfield hareket istemleri

### 01 · Vadi ve sürü

> Begin with a high wide 9:16 view of the approved valley and slowly dolly forward to the supplied matched end. Keep the Count’s three-tower castle fully visible in the upper-right third and the winding path connected to its gate. The small Jiggles play in separate groups, chase each other, balance into a little tower, and briefly slot together then separate. Nobody looks at camera. Keep castle on the hill right background. No text, no morphing of puzzle shape.

### 02 · Gölge

> Start on the exact supplied 01 hold. Continue a small forward push from wide toward medium as a large soft shadow crosses the grass and the light cools. Jiggles stop playing in succession and look uphill, away from camera. A single long silhouette of the Count appears on the path, without revealing his face. Use the provided shadow end. Keep castle on the hill right background. No text, no morphing of puzzle shape.

### 03 · Çuval

> From a low medium Jiggle-height angle, track the approved Count as he loosens the gold cord on the one patched burlap sack already on his shoulder. He grabs the open rim with both hands and sweeps it over the grass in a theatrically smug arc. Small Jiggles roll softly into it. Finish with the sack mouth closing toward and completely covering the lens for natural black. Keep castle on the hill right background. No text, no morphing of puzzle shape.

### 04 · Şatoya tırmanış

> Follow wide from behind at sunset. The Count walks uphill along the approved S-shaped path with the same full patched sack over his shoulder; the gold cord stays tied and the bag wriggles softly. The Count crosses the gate threshold and the two oak leaves close behind him, taking the picture to black. Keep castle on the hill right background. No text, no morphing of puzzle shape.

### 05 · Atölye ve cila

> Use a close overhead push toward the candlelit worktable. Keep the glass jars of colored pieces visible at the left edge, the empty frames against the wall, and the repair pouch, silver thimble and button by the tray. The Count tips the same sack: the rest of the wriggling Jiggles tumble into the glass jars on the left, and he seats exactly four Jiggles in the tray. He polishes them with the single blue-tipped brush; each goes still with a small cold glint. Land on the exact supplied tray end: blue top left, coral top right, lavender bottom left, green bottom right, one continuous meadow picture. In the painted landscape only, keep castle on the hill right background. No text, no morphing of puzzle shape.

### 06 · Çerçeve ve kese

> In a medium candlelit shot, the Count seats the existing ornate gold frame around the assembled picture, gives one short smug presenting gesture toward his own work packs the same brush, thimble and button into the little repair pouch, then leaves it hanging by its leather loop behind the frame. Keep the hook and bag on the rear. Match on the supplied painting close-up and use the picture as the visual anchor for the pullback and cut to the gallery. In the painted landscape only, keep castle on the hill right background. No text, no morphing of puzzle shape.

### 07 · Uzun galeri

> In a wide two-sided night corridor, ease back from the nearest gold frame as the Count finishes hanging Painting #1. Reveal rows of paintings on both walls filled with frozen Jiggles. He walks away into the depth of the hall, and amber sconces go out one after another until only a faint spill remains around Painting #1. Keep the original architecture and carpet stable. In the paintings only, keep castle on the hill right background. No text, no morphing of puzzle shape.

### 08 · Kıpırtı · Video A

> Push slowly from a medium view to a close view of the one faintly lit painting in an almost dark corridor. Only the top-left blue Jiggle blinks and makes a tiny movement; the other three pieces stay frozen. Stop on the exact supplied hold. No hand, tap marker, title or button inside the image. In the painting only, keep castle on the hill right background. No text, no morphing of puzzle shape.

### 09 · Uyanış · Video B

> Start on the exact 08 hold after the player taps or the five-second wait ends. The top-left blue Jiggle wakes and looks alert, still trapped in the tile. Keep coral top right, lavender bottom left and green bottom right frozen. Push optically straight into the same art and level the perspective to land exactly on the supplied level-one board composition. Keep castle on the hill right background in the picture. No text, no morphing of puzzle shape.

### 10 · İlk kurtarış

> At first-level success, tilt the camera down from just above the carpet as the same gold frame is lowered from the wall. Exactly four freed Jiggles leap out and land with a soft bounce. The uncostumed blue protagonist lands ahead and its coral, purple and green companions look toward it, never the viewer. The same leather repair pouch slips from behind the frame and spills the blue-tipped wooden brush, silver thimble and one ivory four-hole button separately onto the burgundy carpet. Keep castle on the hill right background only if it is visible inside surviving paintings. No text, no morphing of puzzle shape.

### 11 · Fırça şövalyesi

> Start medium and low on the blue Jiggle reaching toward the three objects on the carpet. The same Jiggle picks up the blue-tipped brush, puts on the silver thimble and raises the four-hole button shield. Push in briefly as it looks toward the next paintings, then meets the camera with quiet determination once, matching the supplied hero insert. The coastal picture in the next bay is the next painting; the cleared frame remains out of this shot. Pull back to the exact corridor-v04 menu end image as the next painting glows. Keep castle on the hill right background only if it is visible inside a painting. No text, no morphing of puzzle shape.

## Süreklilik ve oyunla birleşim

- **01 → 02** arasında iki dosya bayt bayt aynı. 02’nin ayrı bitiş karesi gölgeyi ve silueti gösterir. 03 alttan, 04 arkadan ve gün batımında. Şato bütün dış karelerde sağ üstte görünür. 03’te alçak Kont planı nedeniyle diğer dış karelerden daha küçük görünür.
- **Tablo #1** tek bir [2×2 kaynak görsel](cekim-kareleri-v06/tablo-01.png). 05’teki tepsi, 06’daki çerçeve, 07–09’daki tablo ve 09’un tahta eşleme karesi bu görselden hazırlanır. Renk sırası sol üst mavi, sağ üst mercan, sol alt mor, sağ alt yeşil. Işık kademesi ve 08 sonundaki mavi Jiggle göz kırpışı sahneye göre değişir. 06 → 07 aynı tabloyla atölyeden geniş galeriye eşleşen kesmedir.
- **08 → 09** arasında [08’in donma karesi](cekim-kareleri-v06/08-bitis.png) ve [09’un başlangıcı](cekim-kareleri-v06/09-baslangic.png) bayt bayt aynı. Oyunda **Uyandır! / Tap to wake!** görünür. Tek dokunuş veya 5 sn bekleme sonunda 09 oynar. 09’un sonu oyun için [tahta eşleme karesi](cekim-kareleri-v06/09-bitis-tahta.png).
- **10 → 11** ilk bölüm kazanılınca oynar. 06’da çerçeve arkasına takılan askılı deri kese 10’da düşer. Aynı mavi uçlu fırça, gümüş yüksük ve dört delikli düğme 11’de kuşanılır. 11 için [kararlı bakış ara karesi](cekim-kareleri-v06/11-kahraman-bakis.png) ve [menü bitişi](cekim-kareleri-v06/11-bitis-menu.png) ayrı.
- **Son yazı** için önceki tercihle **Save the Jiggles! / Jiggles’ı kurtar!** kullanıldı. 11’in kararlı bakışında oyun metin katmanı olarak görünür. Düğme ve son yazı storyboard üzerinde temsili, Higgsfield kareleri yazısız.
- **Menü bitişi** onaylı [corridor-v04](../../../Assets/Art/Menu/corridor-v04-source.png) geometrisini, gece renk düzenini ve merkezdeki sonraki tablo için altın parıltıyı kullanır. Bu kare ile tahta bitişi görsel eşleme hedefleridir.
- **Görsel kaynaklar** [çayır ve şato](mekan-cayir-sato.png), [atölye](mekan-atolye.png), [aksesuarlar](aksesuar.png), [Kont pozları](kont-pozlar.png) ve [kahraman](kahraman-final.png). Kont’un göründüğü üretimlerde [onaylı karakter kartı](../../../Assets/Art/Characters/Baron-reference-v01.png) da girdi olarak kullanıldı. Yerleşik ImageGen görsel istemleri ve girdi adları ilgili PNG meta verisinde.
