## Üretim

Yerleşik ImageGen aracı, [onaylı Jigo föyü](../task26/kahraman-final.png) üzerinden kullanıldı. Şeffaf kanallar ve üç siluetin kadraj boşluğu kontrol edildi.

```text
Revise the supplied Reverse Solver Jigo model sheet into one clean, production-quality character turnaround with three full-body orthographic views in a wide horizontal canvas. From left to right: straight FRONT, exact right-facing PROFILE, straight BACK. Same identity and scale in all views, equal baseline, generous transparent gutters, no contact shadows. No words, dividers, floor lines, card, scenery, framing or watermark. Keep the established turquoise square jigsaw body, rounded outward tab on his anatomical left, matching opposite notch, short teal limbs, large warm brown eyes, small friendly smile, silver dimpled thimble helmet and exact creamy four-hole button shield in anatomical left hand. Give him a small old-style handmade warrior outfit, a short dark marine cloth tabard with a narrow muted-cream hem across the lower body, a tiny warm brown leather belt and simple leather bracers. Keep the head and both jigsaw edges completely readable. His right hand carries the same gold-ferruled, blue-tipped wood paintbrush from the original, with the wooden shaft slightly longer and blue paint tapering into a soft pointed spear-like tuft. Preserve this recognizable improvised brush-spear, no metal blade. In the front view his brush is on viewer left and shield on viewer right, reversed in the back view. Match the reference's soft, tangible 3D toy rendering, brushed silver, restrained amber edge light and turquoise palette. Complete all three figures including toes and brush tips with clear space around every edge. True transparent background.
```
